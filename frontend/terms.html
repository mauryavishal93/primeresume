SERVICE INFORMATION

Terms of Service

These terms describe the current ResumeOut.com service, the free editor, and how Pro access is managed when billing is enabled.

1. Service overview

ResumeOut.com provides a browser-based resume editor with 25 templates (six available in Free), PDF preview and export, resume-file import, JSON backup, ATS checks, editable cover-letter drafts, optional Google account cloud resumes, public PDF sharing, community features, and a referral program. Pro adds access to all templates, unlimited Pro-template PDF downloads, and job-description ATS comparison. We provide the service on an as-is basis and may change templates, features, or limits over time.

2. Free and Pro features

Core resume editing features remain available without payment or sign-in. Free users can create and edit resumes, use guest browser saving, export standard PDFs, import/export JSON backups, run resume-only ATS checks, and use basic cover-letter tools. A signed-in account gets two lifetime PDF downloads using Pro templates. Active Pro access adds all templates, unlimited Pro-template PDF downloads, and job-description ATS comparison. Other features and limits are listed on the Plans page.

When billing is enabled, Pro is a recurring paid subscription and the current price is shown on the Plans page before checkout. The active subscription renews on the displayed billing cycle until canceled. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues through the current paid period.

3. Data you create in the editor

Resume content, notes, job descriptions, skills, education, and any generated cover-letter draft are user-created content. These remain your data. We store only the information needed to operate the editor, save browser or cloud drafts, and provide the requested service. You remain responsible for reviewing and checking the content before you send it to employers or third parties.

4. Browser save and cloud save

When you are not signed in, the editor's Save in this browser toggle controls whether your current project is autosaved in local browser storage. If it is off, the current draft remains in memory and is discarded when the page is refreshed or closed. Signing in with Google saves edits to your account-scoped cloud resume library. The editor reopens the last-worked cloud resume when available and otherwise opens the most recently updated one.

Public PDF sharing is optional. Anyone with a link you create can view and download that shared resume; you can manage and revoke active share links in the editor.

We do not guarantee that any locally saved or cloud-saved content will remain accessible indefinitely without user action. We recommend keeping your own backup of important projects.

5. AI, ATS, and generated outputs

ATS checks and job-description comparisons are estimates intended to help you review resume structure and role fit. They do not guarantee a particular score, ATS compatibility, recruiter approval, interview, or hiring outcome. Cover-letter drafts are starting points based on the information you provide; review and edit them before use.

6. Billing, payments, and support

Purchases are processed by Razorpay when checkout is enabled. Razorpay handles payment transactions in accordance with its own terms and privacy practices. ResumeOut.com stores billing information needed to manage entitlements and account access. Please see the Privacy Policy, Refund Information, and Support page for more information. Contact resumeout@gmail.com for support.

7. Referral rewards

When the Referral & Rewards program is available, a referral is attributed to the first valid referral link opened within the preceding 30 days. The referred person must be a genuinely new ResumeOut account, pass server-side eligibility checks, and have only one referral attribution. Existing accounts, self-referrals, duplicate attributions, rejected referrals, and suspected abuse are not eligible. We may review, reject, or disable referrals and referral codes to prevent misuse.

After an eligible new account is verified, both the referrer and referred user may receive 7 promotional Pro days. The referrer may receive one additional reward after that referred user's first verified successful paid Pro subscription payment: 7 days for the monthly plan or 30 days for the yearly plan. Visits, checkout starts, failed or pending payments, renewals, later plan changes, and unverified payment events do not qualify. Referral days are promotional, non-cash, non-transferable, and stack after existing paid access and earlier valid referral rewards.

Disabling auto-renewal while a paid period remains active does not revoke a conversion reward. If a subscription is cancelled immediately and Razorpay confirms it is no longer active, or if the qualifying payment is confirmed refunded or charged back, we may revoke only the linked paid-conversion reward. Registration rewards, other referrals, independently purchased Pro access, vouchers, and unrelated administrator-granted access are not revoked by that event. Any reversal is recorded separately in the reward ledger. Referral rewards do not change subscription plans, prices, payment records, renewal settings, or billing dates.
