SERVICE INFORMATION

Terms of Service

These terms describe the current ResumeOut.com service, the free editor, and how Pro access is managed when billing is enabled.

1. Service overview

ResumeOut.com provides a browser-based resume editor, resume templates, preview/export tools, saved projects, optional Google sign-in, cloud resume storage, ATS-ready guidance, and cover-letter drafting features. The site is designed for resume creation, improvement, and final PDF export. We provide the service on an as-is basis and may change templates, features, or limits over time.

2. Free and Pro features

Core resume editing features remain available without payment or sign-in. Free users can create and edit resumes, use the guest browser-save toggle, export standard PDFs, import/export JSON backups, and use the basic editing and cover-letter tools. Pro access adds additional templates, deeper job-description guidance, and expanded PDF/export capability when the active server configuration allows it.

When billing is enabled, Pro is a recurring paid subscription and the current price is shown on the Plans page before checkout. The active subscription renews on the displayed billing cycle until canceled. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues through the current paid period.

3. Data you create in the editor

Resume content, notes, job descriptions, skills, education, and any generated cover-letter draft are user-created content. These remain your data. We store only the information needed to operate the editor, save browser or cloud drafts, and provide the requested service. You remain responsible for reviewing and checking the content before you send it to employers or third parties.

4. Browser save and cloud save

When you are not signed in, the editor's Save in this browser toggle controls whether your current project is autosaved in local browser storage. If it is off, the current draft remains in memory and is discarded when the page is refreshed or closed. Signing in with Google changes the save path to your account-scoped cloud resume library. The latest-updated resume in your account opens automatically when available.

We do not guarantee that any locally saved or cloud-saved content will remain accessible indefinitely without user action. We recommend keeping your own backup of important projects.

5. AI, ATS, and generated outputs

ATS guidance, keyword checks, and generated drafts are helper tools intended to improve alignment with a role and reduce obvious gaps. They are not guarantees of hiring decisions, recruiter approval, or compatibility with any specific ATS or employer system. Generated cover-letter text and recommendations should be checked and edited before use.

6. Billing, payments, and support

Purchases are processed by Razorpay when checkout is enabled. Razorpay handles payment transactions in accordance with its own terms and privacy practices. ResumeOut.com stores only the subscription status and account information needed to manage entitlements and account access. Please see the Privacy Policy, Refund Information, and Support page for more information. Contact buildymyportfolio@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp for help.
