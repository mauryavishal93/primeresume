Typical issues we can help with

  • Resume export or PDF preview issues
  • Importing a PDF, DOCX, TXT, JSON, or image resume
  • Browser saving, Google sign-in, or cloud resume access
  • Templates, resume-only ATS checks, job-description matching, or cover-letter drafts
  • Public resume links, community posts, or referral rewards
  • Plan access, upgrades, or billing questions
  • Refund review requests and payment discrepancies

What to include

  • The email address used to sign in with Google (for account or billing help).
  • What you were trying to do, what happened, and the page or browser/device you used.
  • For payments, the plan, approximate date, amount and currency, and Razorpay payment or subscription ID if available.
  • For imports, the file type and any displayed error. Please do not email passwords, card details, or a resume containing personal information unless support specifically asks for it.
