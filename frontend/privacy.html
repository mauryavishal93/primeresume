YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ResumeOut.com handles information when you use the resume editor, browser or cloud saving, community, public PDF links, referrals, and billing features.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

ResumeOut.com is a browser-based resume editor powered by ResumeOut. This policy applies to the website, editor, cloud resume and public sharing features, community, referrals, and billing. If you have a privacy question, contact resumeout@gmail.com.

2. Information you create in the editor

The editor lets you create resumes, add work history, education, skills, and projects, and generate cover-letter text. This information is user-created content and is stored only as needed to provide the service. If you are not signed in, the editor may keep the current project in your browser's local storage when Save in this browser is enabled. If the toggle is off, the project remains in memory and is discarded on refresh or close.

If you sign in with Google, your Google account ID, email address, and display name may be used for authentication and account identification. Resume projects may be saved in Firebase under your account so you can reopen them later. Resume projects are not used for unrelated marketing purposes.

3. Cloud storage and account features

Cloud storage is optional and is used for account-scoped resume management. The site stores the last-worked resume preference so the editor can reopen it; if it is unavailable, the most recently updated resume may open. Your account profile can store your display name, optional mobile number, highest education, company, and designation; your sign-in email is read-only. Company names saved in your profile are shown with your name on Community posts. Highest education and designation remain in your account profile and are not displayed with posts. If you use the Community or My posts area, public posts and comments may include the content you write and your display name, with account identifiers in private records for moderation.

We keep private moderation records so admin tools can review community activity and maintain accountability. Public community content is visible to anyone viewing the feed; private moderation data is not shown in the public feed.

If you create a public PDF share link, anyone with that link can view and download the shared resume. You can manage and revoke active share links in the editor. Avoid sharing a link if you do not want its resume content to be public to people who receive it.

4. Payment and subscription information

When checkout is enabled and you purchase a Pro plan, Razorpay processes the payment. ResumeOut.com receives only the billing data needed to manage access, such as your account ID, selected plan, billing status, and renewal date. Resume content is not sent to Razorpay as part of the checkout. We use Firebase to store the subscription status against your signed-in account.

5. Technical and usage data

To keep the site secure and functioning, hosting and Firebase services may process technical data such as IP addresses, browser information, request metadata, and account activity logs. A functional, HttpOnly referral-attribution cookie may be set after a referral link is opened; it supports first-touch referral eligibility and expires after 30 days. It is not used to store resume content. We do not sell personal information.

6. Your choices and contact

You can use the editor without signing in, disable browser autosave, delete cloud resumes using the editor controls, revoke active public share links, or delete community content you own. Signing out does not remove cloud resumes from your account. Referral records may include referral codes, account identifiers, eligibility decisions, reward history, and qualifying payment or reversal status so the program can prevent duplicate claims and manage rewards. For privacy, billing, or account questions, contact resumeout@gmail.com or use the Support page.

7. Sharing and third-party services

Google and Firebase process sign-in information and the resume data you choose to store in your account. Razorpay processes payments when you purchase a Pro plan. Hosting providers may process technical request information. If you open an external sharing or payment link, that service's privacy policy applies. We do not sell or rent personal information.

8. Retention and security

Guest resumes remain in browser storage until you turn saving off, remove them, or your browser clears site data. Turning browser saving off removes the stored guest resume. Cloud resumes remain in Firebase until you delete them from the editor; signing out or clearing browser data does not delete them. Export a JSON backup if you want to keep a separate copy. Deleting a cloud resume does not delete a downloaded JSON file or messages you already sent. Protect your device and browser profile, especially on shared devices. No storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children and applicable rights

ResumeOut.com is a general-purpose resume tool and is not directed to children. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights regarding personal information processed by website hosting, Firebase, or in communications you send. Contact us to ask about a request; information stored only in your browser can be managed through your browser settings, and cloud resumes can be managed in the app.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website or its data practices change. The latest version and update date will be published on this page. Please review it periodically.
