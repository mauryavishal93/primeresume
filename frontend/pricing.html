FeatureFreePro

Pricing, purchase, and access terms

Pro is a recurring subscription billed in Indian rupees (INR). The selected plan price is charged at checkout: ₹99 monthly or ₹399 yearly. If you already have voucher or referral Pro access, that price prepays your first subscription period, which begins after your current promotional access ends; recurring billing begins after the prepaid period. The subscription then renews at the displayed price and interval until canceled. Canceling stops future renewals, and access continues through the current paid period. Payments are handled by Razorpay. Resume content is not sent to Razorpay.

Purchases require signing in with Google. Resume editing, guest browser saving, Google account cloud resumes, JSON import/export, resume-only ATS checks, basic cover-letter tools, and standard PDF export are included in Free. Each signed-in account gets two lifetime downloads using Pro templates at no cost; every Pro-template PDF download counts, including repeat downloads. Pro removes that download limit while the subscription is active.
