How rewards work

  • After a genuinely new referred user registers and passes eligibility checks, both of you receive 7 promotional Pro days.
  • After their first verified successful monthly Pro payment, you receive 7 more days; for a yearly payment, you receive 30 more days.
  • Checkout starts, failed payments, renewals, later plan changes, and unverified payment events do not earn a conversion reward.
  • Turning off auto-renewal does not remove a conversion reward while the paid period remains active. A verified immediate cancellation, refund, or chargeback can reverse only that conversion reward. Registration rewards and rewards from other referrals remain.
  • Referral Pro days are promotional, non-cash, non-transferable, and stack after existing paid access and earlier valid referral rewards.
